# Riverside General Hospital: Formulary: Antibiotics

Effective September 1, 2026. Subject to change.

- First-line is Amoxatrin 1 g three times daily for 5 days.
- Halve the Cefalyn dose when eGFR falls below 30 mL/min.
- Meropax and Linezorin need infectious-disease approval within 24 hours.
- Switch to oral after 48 hours if the patient is afebrile and eating.
