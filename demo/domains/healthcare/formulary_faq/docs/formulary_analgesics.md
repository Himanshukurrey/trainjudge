# Riverside General Hospital: Formulary: Analgesics

Effective September 1, 2026. Subject to change.

- Adults take 20 mg of Zolvarin once daily; the formulary limit is 40 mg a day.
- Pentacor is capped at 3 g per day across all doses, 2 g for patients over 75.
- Relvix is given every 8 hours, with at least 6 hours between doses.
- Children aged 6 to 12 take 0.3 mg/kg of Zolvarin, up to 10 mg a day.
