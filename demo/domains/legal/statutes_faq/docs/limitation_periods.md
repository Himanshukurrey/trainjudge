# State of Westmark (fictional): Limitation Periods

Effective September 1, 2026. Subject to change.

- Contract claims must be filed within 6 years of the breach under Westmark Civil Code §4-210.
- Personal injury claims must be filed within 3 years of the injury.
- Defamation claims must be filed within 1 year of publication.
- The limitation period is paused until the claimant turns 18.
