# Cobaltworks (fictional): Pay and Reviews

Effective September 1, 2026. Subject to change.

- Pay is reviewed every April, effective from May 1.
- Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month.
- Overtime above 40 hours a week is paid at 1.5 times the hourly rate.
- A successful employee referral earns a $2,000 bonus after 90 days.
