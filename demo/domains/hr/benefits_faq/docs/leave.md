# Cobaltworks (fictional): Leave Policy

Effective September 1, 2026. Subject to change.

- Full-time employees get 25 days of annual leave plus public holidays.
- Parents get 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave in the first year.
- Employees get 10 paid sick days a year; a note is needed after 3 days in a row.
- Up to 5 unused leave days carry over and expire on March 31.
