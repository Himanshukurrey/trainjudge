# Northstar Bank: Personal Loans

Effective September 1, 2026. Rates and charges are subject to change.

- Personal loans are offered at 10.99% to 18.50% p.a., depending on credit score and income.
- Personal loans can be foreclosed after 12 EMIs with a 3% charge on the outstanding principal.
- Personal loans go up to ₹25 lakh for salaried customers earning at least ₹30,000 a month.
- A late EMI attracts a penal charge of 2% per month on the overdue amount.
