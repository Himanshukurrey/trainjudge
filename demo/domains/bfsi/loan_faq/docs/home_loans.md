# Northstar Bank: Home Loans

Effective September 1, 2026. Rates and charges are subject to change.

- Northstar Bank home loans start at 8.35% p.a. for salaried borrowers. The rate is linked to the RBI repo rate and resets every 3 months.
- The home loan processing fee is 0.50% of the loan amount, capped at ₹10,000 plus GST.
- There is no prepayment or foreclosure charge on floating-rate home loans taken by individuals.
- Home loans are available for up to 30 years, ending by the borrower's 70th birthday.
