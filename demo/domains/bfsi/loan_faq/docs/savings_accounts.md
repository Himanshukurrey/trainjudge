# Northstar Bank: Savings Accounts

Effective September 1, 2026. Rates and charges are subject to change.

- Regular savings accounts need an average monthly balance of ₹10,000 in metro branches and ₹5,000 elsewhere.
- Savings balances up to ₹5 lakh earn 3.00% p.a.; balances above ₹5 lakh earn 3.50% p.a.
- Falling short of the minimum balance costs 5% of the shortfall, up to ₹500 a month.
- Customers get 5 free withdrawals a month at Northstar ATMs and 3 at other banks' ATMs in metro cities.
