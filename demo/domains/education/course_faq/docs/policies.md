# Westbrook University (fictional): Course Policies

Effective September 1, 2026. Subject to change.

- Late coursework loses 5% per day, up to 5 days, then scores zero.
- Students must attend at least 80% of seminars to sit the exam.
- Extensions of up to 7 days need a request before the deadline.
- Suspected misconduct is reviewed by a panel within 15 working days.
