# Northstar Bank: KYC Requirements

Effective September 1, 2026. Rates and charges are subject to change.

- Accepted KYC documents are Aadhaar, passport, voter ID and driving licence, along with PAN or Form 60.
- Low-risk customers must complete re-KYC every 10 years; high-risk customers every 2 years.
- New customers can complete Video KYC with a bank official in about 10 minutes using Aadhaar and PAN.
- Accounts opened with OTP-based e-KYC are limited to a ₹1 lakh balance until full KYC is completed, within 1 year.
