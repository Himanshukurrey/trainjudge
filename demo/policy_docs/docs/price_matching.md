# Brightlane Price Matching

Effective March 1, 2026.

- We match a lower price on an identical item within 14 days of purchase.
- Price matching applies only to authorized U.S. retailers with the item in stock; marketplace sellers are excluded.
- If Brightlane lowers its own price within 14 days of your purchase, we refund the difference.
- Each customer may make up to 3 price-match requests per calendar month.
- Clearance and open-box items are not eligible for price matching.
- Price-match differences are refunded to the original payment method within 2 business days.
