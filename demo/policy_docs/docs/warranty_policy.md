# Brightlane Warranty Policy

Effective March 1, 2026.

- Furniture is covered by a 5-year limited warranty against structural defects.
- Small appliances carry a 2-year limited warranty from the date of delivery.
- The warranty does not cover normal wear, accidental damage, or damage from commercial use.
- Warranty claims are filed from the Order History page with a photo of the defect; claims are reviewed within 3 business days.
- Warranties are non-transferable and apply only to the original purchaser.
- The Brightlane Care plan extends any warranty by 3 years and costs 12% of the item's purchase price.
