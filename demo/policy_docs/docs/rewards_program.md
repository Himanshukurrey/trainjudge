# Brightlane Brightlane Rewards

Effective March 1, 2026.

- Brightlane Rewards members earn 2 points per dollar spent; Gold members earn 3 points per dollar.
- Every 100 points is worth $1 off a future order.
- Points expire 12 months after they are earned if the account has no purchases in that period.
- Members reach Gold status after spending $1,000 in a calendar year.
- Gold members receive a $25 birthday reward, valid for 30 days.
- Points are not earned on gift card purchases, shipping fees, or taxes.
