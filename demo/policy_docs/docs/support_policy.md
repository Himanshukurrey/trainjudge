# Brightlane Customer Support

Effective March 1, 2026.

- Phone support is available Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time, and Saturday 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- Live chat aims to connect customers with an agent within 2 minutes during support hours.
- Email tickets receive a first response within 24 hours, including weekends.
- Customers can request escalation after 2 unresolved contacts; escalated tickets go to a senior specialist within 1 business day.
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge within 1 hour of being placed; after that they can only be returned once delivered.
- Account changes require a one-time code sent to the email on file; agents never ask for a full password.
