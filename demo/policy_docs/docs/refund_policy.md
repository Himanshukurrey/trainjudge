# Brightlane Refund Policy

Effective March 1, 2026.

- Unopened items can be returned within 45 days of delivery for a full refund.
- Opened electronics can be returned within 15 days of delivery and are subject to a 10% restocking fee.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item reaches our warehouse.
- Final-sale items, marked with a red tag on the product page, cannot be returned or refunded.
- Brightlane pays return shipping for defective or incorrect items; for all other returns a $7.95 label fee is deducted from the refund.
- Without an order number or receipt, returns are accepted for store credit only, at the item's lowest price in the last 90 days.
